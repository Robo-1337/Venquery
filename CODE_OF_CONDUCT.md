# Code of conduct

Venquery is a small project and we'd like it to stay a friendly one.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Everyone here started as a beginner at something.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Help newcomers find their way instead of pointing them at the door.

If someone's behaviour is making the project worse for others, report it privately through the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form (mark it as a conduct report) and it will be looked at. Maintainers may
remove comments, issues or contributors that don't follow these guidelines.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
